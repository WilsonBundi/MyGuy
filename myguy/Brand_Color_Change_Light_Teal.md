# MyGuy Brand Color Change - Light Teal/Turquoise

## Overview

MyGuy's brand color has been updated from **Purple/Violet** to **Light Teal/Turquoise** to create a fresh, modern, and light identity that doesn't resemble Safaricom or any other competitor.

## Brand Identity Impact

### Light Teal/Turquoise Represents:
- **Freshness** - Modern and clean aesthetic
- **Clarity** - Clear communication
- **Growth** - Like social connections growing
- **Calmness** - Peaceful and welcoming
- **Innovation** - Forward-thinking platform

### Fuchsia/Purple Accent Represents:
- **Creativity** - Original content and ideas
- **Energy** - Active and vibrant community
- **Uniqueness** - Standing out from competitors
- **Highlight** - Drawing attention to important elements

## Compatibility

The light teal/turquoise color palette:
- ✅ Maintains excellent contrast ratios
- ✅ Works well in both light and dark modes
- ✅ Distinct from competitors (not blue like Facebook, not green like WhatsApp/Safaricom)
- ✅ Professional and modern
- ✅ Accessible for all users
- ✅ Light and fresh aesthetic
- ✅ No blue colors anywhere in the application
