# MyGuy Brand Color - Orange

## Overview

MyGuy's brand color is **Orange** - chosen as the best color for an international social app to attract more users.

## Why Orange is Perfect for MyGuy

### 1. **Attention-Grabbing**
- Orange is the most attention-grabbing color after red
- Perfect for social engagement and user attraction
- Stands out in crowded social media market

### 2. **Proven Success**
- Instagram successfully uses orange in their brand
- Massive user attraction and engagement
- Recognized as a successful social platform color

### 3. **Warm & Friendly**
- Creates a welcoming, energetic atmosphere
- Encourages interaction and engagement
- Positive emotional response

### 4. **Universal Appeal**
- Positive associations across cultures
- Represents energy, enthusiasm, creativity
- Works well internationally

### 5. **Differentiation**
- Stands out from blue competitors (Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn)
- Different from green (WhatsApp, Safaricom)
- Unique in the social media space

### 6. **Modern & Trendy**
- Current design trend
- Appeals to younger demographics
- Fresh and contemporary

### 7. **Encourages Action**
- Psychologically stimulates engagement
- Encourages activity and participation
- Call-to-action friendly

### 8. **Memorable**
- High visibility makes brand memorable
- Distinctive and recognizable
- Strong brand identity
